Frequently Asked Question

How do I upload notes?

Log in to your account and go to the “Upload” section. Click on “Choose File” to select your document, add a title and description, and then click “Submit” to upload your notes.

Is NoteSwap free?

Yes, NoteSwap is free to use with essential features. However, we offer premium plans with additional benefits like extra storage and advanced features for a subscription fee.

How do I find specific notes?

Use the search bar located at the top of the homepage or within the “Notes” section. Enter relevant keywords, and you can also filter by tags or subjects to quickly find what you’re looking for.

Can I work on notes with others?

Yes, you can share your notes with other users. Simply open the note, click “Share,” enter their email addresses, and set permissions to allow them to view or edit the notes.

What if I forget my password?

Click the “Forgot Password” link on the login page and enter your registered email address. You will receive an email with a link to reset your password and regain access to your account.

Is my data safe on NoteSwap?

Yes, NoteSwap uses encryption to protect your data during transmission. We also store your information on secure servers and perform regular backups to keep it safe.
