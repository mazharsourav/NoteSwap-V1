Why We Built NoteSwap

We noticed that students often struggle to find quality notes during exam time. Our mission began with a simple goal: to create a platform where students can freely and easily exchange study materials with one another.

Our Current Focus

Right now, we’re focused on building a reliable note-sharing ecosystem across major universities. We're expanding subject coverage, improving content quality, and introducing features like verified reviews, premium subscriptions, and intelligent note recommendations.

Our Future Vision

In the future, NoteSwap aims to be the go-to learning companion for students around the globe. With AI-based search, real-time note collaborations, and personalized learning paths — we’re building a smarter way to study.
